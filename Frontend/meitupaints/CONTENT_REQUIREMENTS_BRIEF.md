### ⚠ Claim-accuracy items — resolve BEFORE these appear on the website

Publishing an unverifiable or overstated claim is a legal and reputational risk, and paint claims are regulated. The web team will not publish these until confirmed in writing.

- **A0-Q1 — Founding year.** 2075 B.S. spans **mid-April 2018 to mid-April 2019 AD**. The website must show the AD year (for search engines and non-Nepali readers). Please confirm the exact AD founding date so we write "Since 2018" or "Since 2019" correctly.
- **A0-Q2 — "Yijian".** Is this an individual co-founder or a **Chinese partner company**? If it is a technology/JV partner, that is a strong, tellable story (international technology, Nepali manufacturing) — but it must be described accurately, with the correct legal name and the nature of the relationship. Also: is the brand name "Meitu" derived from Chinese (美涂)? If so, the meaning is worth telling.
- **A0-Q3 — Capacity units.** Consumers and dealers think in **litres**, not tons. 90 tons/month is roughly 65,000–75,000 litres depending on product density. Please confirm (a) the litre equivalent you want published, and (b) whether 90 tons is *installed capacity* or *current monthly output* — we must label it correctly.
- **A0-Q4 — NS certification.** Nepal Standard marks are issued **per product/standard**, not company-wide. Please provide the certificate numbers, the NS standard number(s), the products covered, and scanned copies. We will display these as verifiable credentials.
- **A0-Q5 — "Iron-free".** This is not a standard industry claim and we cannot interpret it safely. Do you mean free of iron-oxide pigments, free of heavy metals generally, or something else? Please supply the **lab test report** that supports both the lead-free and iron-free claims. Until then, neither claim goes on the site.
- **A0-Q6 — Low-VOC.** Your own published interviews say Meitu is *"actively exploring low-VOC formulations."* The website therefore **must not** claim low-VOC products today. We will phrase this as an active R&D commitment. Tell us immediately if any product line is already certified low-VOC.
- **A0-Q7 — The "only in Nepal" claim.** This is your single strongest differentiator and will become the headline of the site. Because it is a superlative, please confirm in writing that Meitu is prepared to state it publicly (it is already in print in Yatra Magazine 2026, which is good supporting evidence).

## PART B — Photography brief

**Universal technical specs (please pass to the photographer):**
- Deliver **original full-resolution JPEG or RAW**. Minimum 3000px on the long edge.
- **Never send images through WhatsApp/Viber** — they are compressed to ~800px and are unusable for web. Use Google Drive, WeTransfer, or a hard drive.
- Shoot **both landscape and portrait** versions of every hero-worthy scene. Desktop needs landscape; mobile needs portrait. Our traffic is split evenly, so portrait crops are not optional.
- No text, logos, watermarks, borders, or date stamps baked into images. No stock photography.
- Natural light preferred; avoid heavy filters. Colour accuracy matters more than mood — we are selling colour.
- Provide a plain caption per image: what it is, where, which Meitu product is on the surface.

### B1. Factory & manufacturing — **12–18 images**
Wide shot of the plant exterior with signage · production floor overview · mixing/dispersion machinery in operation · filling and packing line · QC laboratory with technician testing · raw material warehouse · finished goods stacked with Meitu branding · dispatch/loading · 2–3 close-up detail shots (pigment, tinting, a batch label being applied).

### B2. Completed projects — **8–12 projects, 4–6 images each**
This is the single most persuasive asset type and the site currently has none. For each project: one wide establishing shot (landscape), one portrait shot for mobile, 2–3 detail shots showing the finish/texture up close, and ideally one "before" image. Mix of: residential exterior, residential interior, a hotel or restaurant, an institutional building (school/hospital/office), and at least 2 showing **granite/texture finishes** since that's a specialty line.
Required caption data per project: building name, city/district, year, products & colours used, contractor/painter if creditable, client permission status.

### B3. Products — **every SKU family, 2 shots each**
Bucket/pack front-facing on a clean white or light-grey background (square, 2000×2000+) · one angled "hero" shot with soft shadow · for texture products, an additional flat swatch shot of the applied finish at close range. Consistent lighting and angle across the whole range — mismatched product shots are the fastest way to look unprofessional.

### B4. People — **15–20 images**
Founder/leadership portrait (environmental, at the factory or office — not a passport photo) · 2–3 leadership/management portraits · painters at work applying Meitu products (hands, rollers, texture tools — these are the most human, most useful images on the site) · dealer shop fronts with Meitu branding, 4–6 different shops across districts · a dealer serving a customer at the counter · training session or painter certification event · QC technician at work.

### B5. Colour & texture — **for /colors and /textures**
Flat, colour-accurate swatch photography or high-resolution scans of the actual painted finish for each texture code · texture finishes shot at raking light to show relief · 4–6 room scenes per major texture family showing the finish in real interiors.

## PART C — Video brief

The site currently has **zero video**. These are ordered by impact.

1. **Company film, 60–90 seconds** — the factory, the people, the products, the promise. Used on Home and About. Deliver: 1080p or 4K, horizontal 16:9 **and** a vertical 9:16 cut for mobile, with and without on-screen text, plus a 6–10 second silent loop version for the homepage hero.
2. **Product/application demos, 30–60 seconds each, 4–6 videos** — how a granite texture is applied, how putty is skimmed, primer-to-topcoat sequence. These serve painters *and* convince homeowners the finish is achievable. Silent-friendly (assume autoplay muted); add captions.
3. **Painter program film, 45–60 seconds** — a real TTP painter explaining what the program did for their livelihood. Highest-trust asset for the painter audience.
4. **Dealer testimonial, 30–45 seconds, 2–3 dealers** — for the Dealership page.

Specs: no background music baked into the master (supply a music-free version), no burned-in logos except an end card, deliver MP4 (H.264) plus the original master.
